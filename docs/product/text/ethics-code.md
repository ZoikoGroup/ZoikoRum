## 2. CORE ETHICAL PRINCIPLES (FOUNDATIONAL)

Zoikorum operates on six non-negotiable principles:

### 2.1 Human Authority First

Technology assists.
 Policies constrain.
 Humans decide.

No algorithm on Zoikorum:

- Executes contracts autonomously

- Releases funds independently

- Resolves disputes unilaterally

- Approves professional eligibility without human oversight

### 2.2 Fair Access, Not Equal Outcomes

Zoikorum ensures:

- Equal access to opportunity

- Transparent rules

- Consistent enforcement

Zoikorum does not guarantee:

- Equal visibility

- Equal success

- Equal outcomes

Markets require merit, not manipulation.

### 2.3 Transparency Over Convenience

If a system decision affects a user, that user must be able to understand:

- What changed

- Why it changed

- What they can do next

Opaque systems are unethical systems.

### 2.4 Proportional Power

Enforcement is:

- Proportionate to risk

- Escalated gradually

- Reversible where appropriate

- Logged and reviewable

There are no silent punishments.

### 2.5 Evidence Over Assertion

Claims, credentials, rankings, and enforcement actions must be:

- Evidence-based

- Verifiable

- Time-bound

- Auditable

Reputation without evidence is misinformation.

### 2.6 Dignity in All Interactions

Every participant is treated with:

- Respect

- Clarity

- Due process

- Opportunity to respond

No participant is disposable.

## 6. AI & AUTOMATION ETHICS (STRICT)

### 6.1 Where AI May Be Used

AI on Zoikorum may assist with:

- Search relevance

- Profile summarization

- Pattern detection

- Risk flagging

- Operational efficiency

All AI outputs are:

- Non-binding

- Explainable

- Reviewable by humans

### 6.2 Where AI Is Explicitly Prohibited

AI must not:

- Make final engagement decisions

- Approve verification or credentials

- Release or withhold payments

- Resolve disputes

- Impose sanctions autonomously

Human accountability is never delegated.

### 6.3 AI Transparency Requirement

Users must always be able to access:

- “Why am I seeing this?”

- “How was this ranked?”

- “What data influenced this result?”

Black-box decisions are not permitted.
