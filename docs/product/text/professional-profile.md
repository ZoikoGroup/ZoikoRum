## 1. EXECUTIVE INTENT

The Professional Profile Page is the trust-conversion layer of Zoikorum.

Its purpose is to move a buyer from interest to engagement by answering five questions instantly:

- Who is this professional?

- Are they verified and eligible to engage?

- What exactly can they be engaged to do?

- What protections exist if something goes wrong?

- What is the next safe action to take?

This page must:

- Establish credibility in under 3 seconds

- Eliminate ambiguity around verification and eligibility

- Make governance visible without overwhelming

- Support both marketplace speed and enterprise scrutiny

This is not a résumé page.
 This is not a social profile.
 This is a transaction-ready professional identity.
