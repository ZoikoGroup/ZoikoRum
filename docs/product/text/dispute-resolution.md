## 0. EXECUTIVE INTENT

Disputes are inevitable in professional services.
 Failure arises not from disputes themselves, but from unstructured, emotional, opaque, or unenforceable resolution.

Zoikorum’s dispute system exists to:

- Resolve disputes predictably, neutrally, and fairly

- Protect buyers, professionals, and the platform

- Prevent unnecessary legal escalation

- Enforce outcomes automatically and immutably

- Produce audit-grade records suitable for enterprise, regulators, and courts

This is not customer support.
 This is governed dispute infrastructure.

Zoikorum does not decide who is “right.”
 Zoikorum enforces process, evidence, authority, and execution under agreed platform terms and policy profiles.

## 3. DISPUTE TYPES (SYSTEM-CLASSIFIED)

- Payment & Milestone Release

- Professional Conduct

Classification is immutable once the dispute is submitted.

## 4. DISPUTE ENTRY POINTS

Disputes may be initiated from:

- Buyer Dashboard → Engagement → Raise Dispute

- Professional Dashboard → Engagement → Dispute Item

- Milestone Review Screen → Dispute Before Release

- Automated Platform Trigger (missed SLA, repeated rejection, compliance flag)

Guardrails

- No duplicate disputes for the same milestone without new evidence

- Accepted and closed milestones can only be disputed within a policy-defined challenge window

- Conduct and compliance disputes bypass negotiation

## 7. PHASE 1 — DISPUTE INITIATION

### Objective

Capture the issue clearly, neutrally, and structurally.

### Required Inputs

- Dispute category (single-select)

- Affected milestone(s)

- Issue summary (max 300 characters)

- Desired outcome (select one):

- Rework

- Partial release

- Full release

- Partial refund

- Full refund

- Timeline extension

- Engagement termination

### Optional (Collapsed)

- Additional context (max 1,000 characters)

- Early resolution preference

### UX Safeguards

- Character counters

- Plain-language tone guidance

- Save-as-draft

- Confirmation modal before submission

## 8. PHASE 2 — AUTOMATIC STABILIZATION

Triggered immediately on submission:

- Disputed milestone funds frozen

- Acceptance actions disabled

- Both parties notified simultaneously

- Dispute timeline initialized

- Evidence phase opened

No party can act unilaterally after this point.

## 9. PHASE 3 — EVIDENCE COLLECTION

### Objective

Ensure outcomes are based on facts, not narratives.

### Evidence Types

- Contract & scope documents

- Milestone definitions

- Deliverables

- Platform communication logs

- Change orders

- Payment ledger entries

- Third-party documentation (if applicable)

### Rules

- Structured checklist for both parties

- Symmetrical visibility rules

- Submission deadline enforced

- Evidence integrity protected (hash + timestamp)

### UX

- Drag-and-drop uploads

- Inline previews

- Evidence completeness progress bar

- “Request clarification” (limited)

## 10. PHASE 4 — DIRECT RESOLUTION WINDOW

### Objective

Resolve disputes without escalation whenever possible.

### Interface

- Structured resolution proposals

- Side-by-side comparison

- No free-form chat

### Allowed Actions

- Accept

- Counterproposal

- Escalate (locked until eligible)

### Timeframe

- Default: 5 business days (policy-configurable)

## 11. PHASE 5 — MEDIATED REVIEW

Triggered if:

- Direct resolution fails

- Policy mandates mediation

- One party escalates legitimately

### Mediator Role

- Neutral process facilitator

- Evidence reviewer

- Recommendation issuer (binding only if policy allows)

### Outputs

- Written recommendation

- Evidence citations

- Accept / Reject options

- Escalation eligibility

## 12. PHASE 6 — ESCALATION PATHS

Escalation is explicit and irreversible.
