## 0. PURPOSE OF THIS CHARTER

This Charter defines how trust, safety, and governance operate on Zoikorum.

It exists to ensure that:

- All participants understand how the platform protects them

- Governance rules are predictable, explainable, and enforceable

- Accountability is embedded before engagement, not after failure

- Risk is managed systemically, not discretionarily

This Charter is not marketing.
 It is a binding governance commitment that informs platform design, enforcement, and evolution.

## 1. ZOIKORUM’S TRUST PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 Foundational Principle

Trust is not assumed. Trust is demonstrated, verified, and maintained.

Zoikorum is designed on the belief that:

- Discovery without governance creates risk

- Speed without structure creates harm

- Ratings without verification create false confidence

Zoikorum therefore embeds trust into the system itself.

## 3. WHAT ZOIKORUM IS (GOVERNANCE CONTEXT)

Zoikorum is a global marketplace where:

- Individuals and businesses offer professional services

- Buyers discover, evaluate, and engage those services

- Engagements are supported by verification, contracts, and payment protection

Zoikorum does not employ professionals and does not deliver professional services.

Zoikorum provides the infrastructure that governs how engagements occur.

## 5. TRUST TIERS (PUBLIC STANDARD)

Zoikorum uses clearly defined Trust Tiers.

### Tier A — Fully Verified

- Identity verified

- Credentials validated (where applicable)

- Jurisdiction eligibility confirmed

- Enhanced checks where required

- Eligible for all engagements

### Tier B — Verified Identity

- Identity verified

- Credentials may be partial or not required

- Eligible for standard engagements

### Tier C — Discovery

- Profile visible

- Limited engagement eligibility

- Verification in progress or optional

Trust tiers are system-enforced, not cosmetic.

## 6. VERIFICATION PRINCIPLES

Verification on Zoikorum is:

- Evidence-based — documents, registries, third-party sources

- Time-bound — credentials expire and must be renewed

- Contextual — requirements vary by profession and jurisdiction

- Visible — buyers can see verification scope and limits

Zoikorum does not rely on self-attestation alone.

## 7. CREDENTIAL VALIDITY & MONITORING

### 7.1 Continuous Oversight

Zoikorum monitors:

- License expiration

- Certification status

- Insurance coverage

- Jurisdiction eligibility

- Background verification timelines

### 7.2 Enforcement

If a credential lapses:

- Eligibility is recalculated immediately

- Engagement permissions adjust automatically

- Buyers are informed transparently

Expired credentials cannot be hidden.
