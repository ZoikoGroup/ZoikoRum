## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Page Objective

Enable a buyer to go from “I need finance help” to “I’ve shortlisted 3 professionals and requested proposals” in ≤ 5 minutes, with trust signals that feel protective, not bureaucratic.

### Category Page Mandate

- Discovery-first — search + browse dominate

- Strategic depth — specialization taxonomy is visible, not hidden

- Trust without clutter — tiers + verification are understandable at a glance

- Decision support — compare + save + preview reduce paralysis

- Enterprise optionality — present, but never in the way

- Global readiness — jurisdiction & credential realities handled gracefully

### Critical Correction (Applied)

- No vanity trust: star ratings and social popularity are not primary trust mechanisms.

- Trust is signaled via verification, eligibility, engagement structure, and record mechanisms.

- If reviews exist, they are secondary, clearly labeled, and never the primary ranking driver.
